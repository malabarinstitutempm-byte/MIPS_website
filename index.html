Enrollment Process

How To Join Malabar Paramedical

We offer a simplified admission procedure designed to help prospective students secure their seats with minimal hassle.

01

Course Selection

Explore our courses section and choose the program that aligns with your educational background and career goals.

02

Enquiry Submission

Fill out the online enquiry form with your details, or contact our admissions office directly via phone or email.

03

Document Verification

Visit the campus with your academic certificates, identification documents, and passport photos for screening.

04

Confirm Admission

Secure your enrollment and receive your syllabus guide, class schedule, and batch details.

Important Admission Guidelines

Students must note that charges for examination fees, text books, degree/diploma certificates, cultural programs, college day celebrations, study tours, uniforms, and student transportation services are applicable separately in addition to the standard tuition rates.
